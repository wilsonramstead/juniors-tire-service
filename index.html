What they do

Tires, wheels, and the flat you found this morning.

A tire shop that stays a tire shop. New and used tires, and everything else that happens between the rim and the road.

New & used tires

Both, and it is on the banner over the door. Bring the size, or bring the vehicle and let them read the sidewall for you.

Flat & puncture repair

Nails, screws, slow leaks. Where the tire allows it, the repair is a proper patch from the inside — not a plug pushed in from the tread.

Mounting & balancing

Tires mounted on your rims and balanced on the machine in the bay, so the steering wheel is still at seventy.

Oversized & custom wheels

Lifted trucks, mud-terrains, deep-dish 24s. The fitments other shops wave off are a normal afternoon here.

Mobile tire service

The other half of the business name. If the vehicle is not going anywhere, call the shop and say where it is sitting.

Safety standards

Some tires they will not patch.

A proper repair means taking the tire off the rim and patching it from the inside. Once a tire is more than five years old, Junior’s will not do that — the risk of putting an aged tire back on the rim is not one they will hand to a customer. You hear that up front, not after the fact.

A customer wrote the policy down himself, in a review he left about a repair that did go ahead:

“Discovered that you can not patch at tire more than five years old from the inside due to liability concerns from removing the tire off the rim.”

DC H. Google review

Oversized & custom fitment

The wheels nobody else would mount.

Big wheels are their own job. The rim is usually worth more than the rubber going onto it, the sidewall does not want to cooperate, and one slip leaves a mark the owner will see forever. Junior’s takes that work instead of sending it down the road.
